# Animus contexts

## Contexts

- [System authoring](packages/system/CONTEXT.md) — styling inputs, their admitted values, and transform ownership.
- [Extraction](packages/extract/CONTEXT.md) — interpretation of authored styling and the policy for reported failures.

## Relationships

System authoring defines the styling contract that extraction interprets. Build strictness belongs to extraction; prop strictness belongs to system authoring. They describe separate policies.
